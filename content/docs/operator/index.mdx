# Operator Guide

<Video src="https://czjrrypzshezcosigaxe.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/demo/Operator/operator.mp4" title="Operator Overview" />

As an **Operator**, you handle day-to-day weighing and trading operations on the field. You can record transactions, manage produce entries, and generate receipts — all from a streamlined interface.

<Callout title="Login" type="info">
  Operators log in at `/login` with their credentials provided by the Organization Owner.
